About the Firm

Sarmad Law Company

Sarmad Law Company, supervised by Mr. Muhammad Sarmad Awan, Advocate High Court, is a reputable firm specialising in civil, criminal and corporate sector matters since 2014, with a strong reputation for handling tax-related issues. The Firm has earned the satisfaction of over 5,000 clients across civil, criminal and corporate matters, with services spanning tax and revenue cases, legal notices, drafting of civil suits and complaints, preparation of legal documents, and representation before district judiciary and High Courts across Pakistan.

Our Team

Meet the Team

Muhammad Sarmad Awan

Advocate High Court · Managing Director & Professional Tax Consultant

With over 10 years of dedicated legal practice, Mr. Awan has consistently applied his legal, managerial, analytical, negotiation and research skills in service of the legal fraternity of Pakistan. He serves as a Professional Tax Consultant, advising clients on taxation laws, compliance and dispute resolution before revenue authorities and appellate forums, and is a firm believer in nurturing a relationship of respect, integrity and cooperation between the Bench and the Bar.

LL.B — University of Sargodha M.Com — University of South Asia Enrolled, Punjab Bar Council Life Member, Lahore High Court Bar Association Life Member, Lahore Tax Bar Association (M-1503)

Regularly appears before: FBR Commissioner Inland Revenue courts (Sargodha, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Lahore); the Appellate Tribunal Inland Revenue (ATIR), Islamabad; the Appellate Tribunal, Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA), Lahore; and the Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) on tax-related matters. Handles filing of Income Tax, Sales Tax, PRA, SRB, BRA, KPRA and Punjab Agriculture Income Tax returns.
